HELP & SAFETY

A safer way to reunite with your belongings

Use these guidelines when reporting, claiming or collecting an item.

🔐

Protect your information

Do not post passwords, full student numbers, home addresses or financial information. Use a campus-approved contact method where possible.

🧾

Verify ownership

When claiming an item, describe a distinctive feature that is not visible in the public report. Campus staff should verify ownership before handing over valuable items.

📍

Arrange a safe collection

Choose an approved campus collection point, such as a staffed reception or security office. Avoid meeting strangers alone.

💬

Be respectful

Use clear, polite language when contacting someone about an item. Do not publish another person's personal information or accuse someone without evidence.
